Outline
=======

- We initiate the topic by defining molecular evolution and phylogenetics.
- We then discuss basic data types required to conduct molecular evolutionary analyses.
- We introduce the Neutral Theory of Molecular Evolution, the foundation theory for understanding genetic variation within and between species.
- We discuss mutation, one of the major evolutionary forces and the origin of all genetic novelty.
- We introduce the statistical models that seek to describe sequence divergence, including the main mathematical objects in these models.
- We develop intuition about how the statistical models relate to biological processes.
- We discuss how these statistical models are applied to testing hypotheses.
