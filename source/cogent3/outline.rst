Outline
=======

- We introduce the main data formats used in molecular evolutionary analyses.
- We introduce the core ``cogent3`` objects for manipulating biological data.
- We apply ``cogent3`` to simple examination of genetic variation.
- We explore the relationship between genetic variation and protein coding sequence using ``cogent3`` ``GeneticCode`` objects.
- We introduce ``cogent3``apps, which perform discrete tasks ranging from data sampling to performing hypothesis tests.
- We apply ``cogent3`` to performing the statistical modelling and hypothesis testing outlined in the Molecular Evolution topic.
